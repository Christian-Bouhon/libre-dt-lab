Libre DT-Lab — RAW photo workflow and editor
An experimental, open-source laboratory built on darktable.

Libre DT-Lab is a free, non-destructive RAW developer and photo workflow
application for photographers. It is an experimental fork of darktable that
explores new approaches to the photographic workflow and ships original
modules you will not find elsewhere. Everything runs locally on your computer:
no account, no telemetry, no trackers.

WHY LIBRE DT-LAB
• Non-destructive RAW development with a zoomable lighttable and a powerful darkroom.
• Fully colour-managed (ICC/LCMS2), 32-bit floating point processing.
• OpenCL GPU acceleration.
• Free and open source (GNU GPL v3).

ORIGINAL MODULES
• Tone & Texture — multi-scale tonal and contrast processing in scene-referred
  linear RGB with wide-gamut (Rec.2020) support: global contrast centred on
  middle grey, pyramidal local contrast from micro to extended, shadow and
  highlight controls evaluated on a low-frequency base, and chromatic contrast
  (colorimetric and warm/cool).
• 3DCF (3D Colorimetric Film) — global tone mapping built on the ACES 2.0 SSTS
  curve combined with a spectral film-gamut simulation that preserves perceived
  hue. Display targets: sRGB, Rec.2020, Display P3, ProPhoto RGB, Adobe RGB,
  with selectable colour looks and CPU / OpenCL implementations.
• ACES 2.0 Reference Rendering — a faithful implementation of the ACES 2.0
  CAM DRT output transform, working-space agnostic.
• Enhanced Basecurve — the classic curve plus selectable tone-mapping engines
  (kinematic, dynamic, cinematic DRT) with JzAzBz-based highlight protection.
• Tone-mapping selector — switch the active tone mapper (filmic, sigmoid, AgX,
  basecurve, 3DCF, ACES 2.0) in a single click.
• Detachable modules — move any module panel into its own window.

DESIGNED TO COEXIST WITH DARKTABLE
• Native sidecar format: Libre DT-Lab writes "image.ext.lab.xmp" and, when no
  ".lab.xmp" exists, falls back to reading the standard ".xmp". darktable is
  never affected by Libre DT-Lab edits.
• Separate settings and cache directories, independent from darktable.

SUPPORTED FORMATS
RAW and image files including DNG, CR2, CR3, CRW, NEF, NRW, ARW, SR2, SRF, RAF,
ORF, RW2, PEF, MRW, KDC, DCR, plus JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, HEIF/HEIC,
JPEG XL, EXR and more.

PLEASE NOTE
This is experimental software. Use it with care and prefer a separate library.
It is a laboratory for testing new ideas, not an official darktable release.
